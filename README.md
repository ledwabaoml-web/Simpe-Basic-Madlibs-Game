SIMPLE-BASIC-MADLIBS-GAME
I did this to practice input and output statements

HOW IT WORKS

-First, you'll need a story that you'll work on. For instance, it could be a short paragraph of how your day went. I personally wrote a short paragragh of what I was seeing from my bedroom window seat

-From your descriptive paragraph you'll then replace words with their definitive parts of speech. For example, you might replace the word "big" with the part of speech variable {Adjective_1}.

-Make variable input statements prompting the user to enter a required part of speech. For example: Adjective_1 = input("Enter an adjective of size: ")

-Put your descriptive paragraph in a print statement with an f-string containing the variables

-The user output will then be the results of the print statement with all the parts of speech entered by the user
